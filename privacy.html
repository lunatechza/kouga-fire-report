Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-01

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kouga Fire Report web app collects and uses information when you submit a report.

What we collect

  • Report details you enter, such as visible signs, severity, size, spread, nearest town/area, and optional notes.
  • Location information if you choose to share GPS coordinates. If GPS is unavailable, you can enter a nearest town/area instead.
  • Basic device and usage information such as your browser user agent and page URL.

Why we collect it

We use your report to help municipal responders track incidents and cross‑reference incoming reports for operational response and safety.

Legal basis / consent

Submission is voluntary. By sending a report you consent to the collection and use of the information described here.

Where it is sent

  • WhatsApp: to the Kouga call centre number you choose to message.
  • Email: to the Kouga call centre email address.
  • Firebase Firestore: a copy is logged for cross‑referencing and audit purposes.

Retention

Reports are kept for operational and audit purposes. Unless otherwise required, we aim to keep records for 12–24 months.

Sharing

Your report is shared with Kouga Municipality responders and operational staff. We do not sell your information.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure.

Your rights and contact

For municipal data queries, contact callcentre@kouga.gov.za.

For app feedback or technical issues, open a GitHub issue at https://github.com/lunatechza/kouga-fire-report/issues.

Children

This service is not intended for children. If you are under 18, please ask an adult to submit the report.
